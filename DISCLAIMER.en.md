# DriveNX – Diagnostic and liability notices

## Diagnostic limitations

DriveNX evaluates available SMART, NVMe and other device information. Such data may be incomplete, restricted by adapters or controllers, or incorrect. The assessment is a technical indication at the time of the query, **not a guarantee** of drive health, remaining lifetime, reliability or data integrity.

Even a drive reported as healthy may fail without prior warning. An unsuccessful or unavailable query does not necessarily establish a drive defect.

## Backups and use

Appropriate backup and precautionary measures should be independently assessed before diagnostic, repair or data recovery work. Diagnostic results do not replace regular backups. Customer devices require coordination with their responsible owners.
